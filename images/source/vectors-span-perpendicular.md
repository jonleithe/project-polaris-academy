# Perpendicular spanning vectors

Editable source: `vectors-span-perpendicular-white-grid.svg`.
Rendered asset: `../vectors-span-perpendicular-white-grid.png`.

Drawn directly as SVG with the maintainer's approval after image generation
reached its usage limit. White paper, a faint coordinate grid, lightly bowed
pencil-like strokes, and equal axis scales preserve the established style as
closely as possible. Endpoints are exact: v = (2, 1), w = (-1, 2).
The rotation arc runs counterclockwise from v to w. Both vectors have length
sqrt(5), and their dot product is zero.

Regenerate the PNG from the repository root with librsvg:

```sh
rsvg-convert -w 2400 -h 1650 -o images/vectors-span-perpendicular-white-grid.png images/source/vectors-span-perpendicular-white-grid.svg
```

The notes consume the PNG for consistent HTML and XeLaTeX rendering. Edit the
SVG and regenerate the PNG together.
