Any system of linear equations can be solved using elimination with matrices, given that the system is consistent.

Suppose we have the system:

$$
\begin{align}
x + 2y + z &= 2 \\
3x + 8y + z &= 12 \\
4y + z &= 2
\end{align}
$$

We call this system $A\vec{x}=\vec{b}$, where $A$ is the coefficient matrix of the linear system, $\vec{x}$ is a vector of unknows and $\vec{b}$ is the right hand side of the linear system, the constants if you will.
